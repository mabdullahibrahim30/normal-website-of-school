Bahria College NORE-1 in Karachi is an educational institution founded in 1986 to provide quality education, primarily for the children of the Pakistan Navy, but also open to the public. It offers a comprehensive educational program from Montessori to higher secondary levels, including Cambridge O and A levels, and has a focus on both academic excellence and student development. The college emphasizes a stimulating and modern learning environment, and admissions are based on merit and seat availability. About the college Establishment: Founded on August 30, 1986, by Admiral I. A. Sirohey. Mission: To provide quality education with a focus on academic excellence, student development, and moral attributes. Governance: It is an autonomous institution governed by an independent body. Location: M. T. Khan Road, Karachi, Pakistan. Educational offerings Class levels: Offers education from the Montessori level up to Higher Secondary Level. Cambridge Program: Includes both Ordinary Level (O Level) and Advanced Level (A Level) courses from the University of Cambridge. Entry levels: Key entry points are Montessori, Class VI, and Class XI (Pre-Engineering and Pre-Medical). Admissions Admission policy: Admissions are based on merit and seat availability, with priority given to children of Naval personnel and then to the general public. Application process: Involves submitting a registration form and prospectus, followed by an admission test and interview. Eligibility: Applicants must meet age requirements and have a clear record; admission for students transferring from other Bahria Colleges is mandatory. Facilities and activities Facilities: Includes labs and a library with a collection of over 2,600 books. Activities: The college fosters student development through extracurricular activities and aims to develop strong communication and leadership skills
